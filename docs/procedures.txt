STOCK POLICY 
Any product whose stock falls below 10 units must be automatically replenished. 
A stock level of 0 indicates that the product is out of stock. 
Stock shortages must be reported to the sales manager within 24 hours. 

ORDER PROCEDURE 
An order with the status ‘pending’ is awaiting approval from the sales department. 
An order with the status “in progress” is being prepared in the warehouse. 
An order with the status “delivered” has been shipped and received by the customer. 

PRODUCT CATEGORIES 
IT: laptops, desktops, servers. 2-year warranty. 
Peripherals: keyboards, mice, webcams. 1-year warranty. 
Displays: monitors, projectors. 3-year warranty.
