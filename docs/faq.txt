FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - CUSTOMER SERVICE

DELIVERY AND DELIVERY TIMES
Q: What are the delivery times?
A: Orders placed before 2 p.m. are shipped the same day.
Standard delivery time: 2 to 3 business days in mainland France.
Express delivery available (24 hours) with a €15 surcharge.

Q: How can I track my order?
A: A confirmation email with a tracking number is sent as soon as the order is shipped.
Tracking is available on the carrier's website using this number.

WARRANTY AND AFTER-SALES SERVICE
Q: How do I make a claim under the warranty?
A: Contact customer service by email with the order reference and a description of the problem.
Computers: 2-year warranty, replacement or repair within 48 business hours.
Peripherals: 1-year warranty, standard exchange within 5 days.
Display: 3-year warranty, on-site technician available in case of screen defect.

Q: Does the warranty cover accidental damage?
A: No, the standard warranty only covers manufacturing defects.
Optional breakage insurance is available when ordering (+10% of the product price).

ORDERS AND PAYMENT
Q: Can I modify or cancel an order?
A: A pending order can be modified or canceled at no cost.
An order that is "in progress" can no longer be modified, but can be returned within 14 days.
An order that has been delivered is subject to a 14-day right of withdrawal (unopened product).

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: Bank transfer (2-day delay), credit card (immediate), check (5-day delay).
Payment in 3 installments with no fees available for orders over 300 euros.

STOCK AND AVAILABILITY
Q: What does the status "out of stock" mean?
A: The product is temporarily unavailable. Replenishment is automatically triggered.
Usual delivery time: 5 to 10 business days depending on the supplier.
It is possible to register on a waiting list to be notified in advance.

Q: Is the stock displayed in real time?
A: Yes, the inventory is updated in real time with each validated order and each
receipt of goods in the warehouse.
